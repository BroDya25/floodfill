📖 Rules of the game

The goal of the game is to paint the entire playing field one color in a limited number of moves.
1
The game starts in the upper left corner. This corner is your "zone", which is gradually expanding.
2
Choose a color from the palette at the bottom of the playing field. All cells in your zone will change to the selected color.
3
If neighboring cells have the same color, as well as your zone - they join it.
4
Fill in the entire field with one color before you run out of moves - and you win!
5
If the move limit is reached and the field is not yet filled — the game is lost. Click "🔄 New Game" to try again.

Colors

There are 7 colors. Choose the one that will "capture" neighboring cells the most.

💡 Advice

Start with the color that is most around your zone - this way you will capture more cells in one move and save moves!
